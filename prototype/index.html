Design notes
  • Ad gating: Banner on Home (3) and Library (14); Interstitial after Save/Export (11). All controlled by one PlanStatus flag; Premium (16) removes them.
  • Privacy: Everything shown is stored locally; no network screens except model download (19) and AdMob.
  • RTL: Screen 17 and chat bubbles must mirror layout for Arabic content.
  • Long operations (export, OCR, indexing, model download) always show progress and are cancelable.
