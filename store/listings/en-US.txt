title: Doculoom: PDF Scanner & Tools
short: Scan documents, edit and sign PDFs, compress and share. Private, works offline.
---
Doculoom turns your phone into a document scanner and a toolbox for PDFs, Office files and photos. Your documents stay on your phone: there is no account to create, and scanning, text reading and the PDF tools all work offline.

SCAN
• Scan pages with the camera and let Doculoom find the edges, or import photos, PDFs and Word files
• Multi-page scanning, re-crop, rotate, reorder pages and apply a filter to every page
• Filters for text, colour and black and white
• Scan modes for documents, receipts, business cards and IDs
• Read the text in a scan (OCR) on the device, search it later, copy it or turn a business card into a contact

PDF TOOLS
• Compress, merge, split, crop and number pages
• Lock a PDF with a password or unlock one you can open
• Fill in forms, draw, add text and sign documents with your saved signature
• Cover private details with the redact tool
• Compare two PDFs and see what changed
• Read PDF and Word files without importing them

OFFICE AND PHOTO TOOLS
• Open, edit and convert Excel, CSV and PowerPoint files
• Table to Excel from a photo, and receipts to a spreadsheet
• Passport and ID photos in the right size for about 40 countries
• Compress photos to a target size, add a watermark, remove hidden data from photos
• Copy both sides of an ID card onto one page

QR AND SHARING
• Scan and create QR codes and barcodes, with a history you control
• Share a PDF as it is, smaller, with a password, or in a ZIP. Only the copy you share changes
• Share a file by QR code over the same Wi-Fi, with an optional password

INVOICES, CV AND REMINDERS
• Invoice maker with GST (India), US sales tax and Saudi VAT layouts, and an amount-in-words tool for cheques
• Payment QR codes, VAT refund receipts, and a CV maker with 25 designs
• Hijri calendar and date converter, with Ramadan, Eid and yearly reminders
• Expiry reminders for passports, policies and other documents

CARDS, CHECKLISTS AND PACKETS
• Keep loyalty and club card barcodes on your phone and show them at the till
• Ready-made checklists for moving, a new job, a visa or taxes, with your scans linked to each step
• Application packet: put your papers into one PDF with a cover, contents and page numbers, within a size limit
• Safe ID copy: cover what the other side does not need and add a mark that says what the copy is for
• Stamp library for documents

PRIVACY AND CONTROL
• No sign-in. Documents are stored only in the app's private storage
• Lock a document or folder with your phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock
• Create encrypted backups with a password you choose
• Delete one document, or everything, from Settings at any time
• Available in 20 languages, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Marathi, Urdu, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese and major European languages

ADS AND COINS
The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Scanning, saving, viewing, searching and sharing a PDF are always free. Some tools use coins. You get a few free coins each day, and you can earn more or a time pass by choosing to watch short videos. Coins have no cash value.
