Doculoom Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Last updated: 30 October 2026
Applies to the Doculoom app for Android (package name com.doculoom.app) and to this website page.

The short version

  • Your documents stay on your phone. Scanning, text reading (OCR), PDF tools, signing, photo tools and backups all run on the device.
  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking by us. We cannot see your documents.
  • The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Nothing is uploaded unless you use the optional Share with QR code → Anywhere feature, which uploads one file in encrypted form for a limited time.
  • You are in control: delete any document, delete everything with one tap (Settings → Privacy → Delete all my data), or uninstall the app.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what information the Doculoom app accesses, what leaves your phone, who receives it and what you can do about it. It covers the app and the optional sharing service the app can connect to. It does not cover other apps, websites or services that you open from Doculoom (for example a link you tap, or the app you share a file to). Those have their own policies.

Doculoom does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other identity information to use the app.

3. Information that stays on your device

The following is created and stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not receive it and cannot see it. It is removed when you delete it in the app, when you use Delete all my data, or when you uninstall the app.

  • Documents and pages: camera photos, imported images, PDFs and Word, Excel, CSV and PowerPoint files, the edited and processed versions, thumbnails, titles, folders, tags, favourites, pins and dates.
  • Recognised text (OCR) saved next to a document so that you can search your library.
  • Tool data you choose to save: signatures, business details and logo for watermarks and invoices (including a bank account or IBAN if you type one), CV details, VAT refund receipts, ID card copies, saved checklists and loyalty or payment QR entries.
  • QR and barcode scan history (you can switch it off or clear it).
  • Settings and preferences: language, region, paper size, default filter, export quality and similar choices.
  • Reminders: document-expiry and Hijri-date reminders, stored on the phone and shown as local notifications.
  • Coin balance and pass status used by the reward system (see section 14).
  • Backups: if you create a backup, the file is encrypted with the password you choose before it is written. It leaves your phone only if you share or save it yourself.

Files you create with the tools (for example a merged PDF, a compressed PDF, a ZIP, a password-protected copy) are temporary copies. They are shared or saved only where you choose.

Passwords and certificates

The password you type to lock or unlock a PDF, to open a protected file, to encrypt a backup, to protect a shared link, or the password of a signing certificate (.p12 / .pfx) is used on the phone only to perform that action. It is not stored and not sent anywhere. If you lose a password, nobody, including us, can recover the file.

5. Information we collect

The app itself collects no personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no crash-reporting service of ours and no tracking. We do not sell personal data.

Data leaves your device only in these cases:

  1. Advertising — data collected by Google's advertising SDK, described in section 7.
  2. Google ML Kit diagnostics — see section 6.
  3. On-device language models — when you first use translation or a new text-recognition script, the model may be downloaded from Google. The request is made by Google's library and does not include your documents.
  4. Optional “Share with QR code → Anywhere” — one file you choose to share, encrypted on your phone first (see section 8).
  5. Things you send yourself — an email you write to our support address, or a file you share to another app.

7. Advertising and consent

The free version of Doculoom is supported by ads from Google AdMob. The app shows banner or native ads inside lists, occasional full-screen ads after a finished task, and rewarded videos that you choose to watch to earn coins or a pass. Ads are not shown to Premium users or during an active pass.

What the advertising SDK may collect

To select, show, limit the frequency of, measure and secure ads, Google's SDK may collect and process the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version, network type), and ad interaction data (such as ads shown, clicked and viewed). It may also use identifiers in the app's storage to count ads. This data is collected and used by Google under its own privacy policy. Google acts as an independent data controller for this data.

Your documents, text, photos and file names are never shared with advertisers or passed to the ad SDK. The app does not send keywords or content about your files with ad requests.

Consent

Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. If you do not agree, ads that need consent are not requested, and videos for coins cannot be played. You can still use the free features and any coins or pass you already have.

You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.

Other ways to limit ads

  • Delete or reset your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path differs slightly by phone maker).
  • Manage Google ad personalisation: adssettings.google.com.
  • Watch a pass video series to remove ads for a period (see section 14).

Doculoom is not directed to children, and ads are not requested with child-directed settings (see section 12).

8. Sharing, printing and QR sharing

Nothing leaves your phone unless you share it. Sharing, saving and printing use Android's standard share, save and print screens, so you choose where a file goes. Once a file reaches another app or person, that app or person's privacy practices apply.

Share with QR code → Same Wi-Fi

Starts a small web server on the Wi-Fi network you are connected to. Anyone on the same network who scans the code can download that one file until it expires or you stop sharing. Nothing is uploaded to the internet. A notification is shown while sharing is active, and a copy of the file is kept in the app's private storage only for that time. You can add a password.

Share with QR code → Anywhere (optional internet sharing)

  • The file is encrypted on your phone first. Only the encrypted data is uploaded to our sharing service.
  • The decryption key is placed only inside the link or QR code (or derived from the password you set) and is never sent to us. We cannot read the file.
  • The encrypted file is deleted when the link expires (5 minutes by default, at most 7 days) or when you tap Stop sharing.
  • As with any web service, the service and its hosting provider (Cloudflare) may process technical data such as the IP address and time of an upload or download in order to deliver the file and protect against abuse. The sharing service does not keep a separate access log of its own and does not link requests to a person.
  • Anyone who has the complete link can open the file until it expires. Share links only with people you trust.

Share PDF options

When you tap Share PDF, you can compress the copy, add a password and/or put it in a ZIP. These changes apply only to the copy you share. The saved document in the app does not change.

9. Security

  • Documents are stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unrooted phone.
  • Locked documents and folders require your phone's screen lock or biometrics to open.
  • PDF passwords use AES-256 encryption. Backups are encrypted with ChaCha20-Poly1305 using a key derived from your password (PBKDF2), and any change to a backup file is detected.
  • Internet sharing encrypts the file on your phone before upload, and all connections to the sharing service use HTTPS.
  • We limit what the app can do to what its features need, and we do not include code that collects data for purposes unrelated to the app.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep your phone's screen lock enabled, keep your phone updated, and use strong passwords for backups and protected files.

11. Your choices and rights

Because the app keeps your information on your phone, you already control it directly: you can view, edit, export and delete it inside the app, and you can revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Doculoom → Permissions.

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, California law, or similar laws), you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to withdraw consent, to object to certain processing, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority. For data that we do not hold, we cannot look it up; for data held by Google (advertising), please use Google's privacy tools. If you contact us, we will respond within a reasonable time and in any case within the period required by law.

Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

  • Consent — for ads that require it, and for the camera and notifications permissions you grant.
  • Performance of a service you request — for features you use on your device and for optional sharing.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the optional sharing service secure and preventing abuse.

California and other US states

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising ourselves. Advertising is provided by Google; where you are offered a choice about personalised ads, you can use Ad privacy choices in Settings or Google's ad settings.

12. Children

Doculoom is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where a higher age applies). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the sharing feature or by email, contact us and we will delete it.

13. International data transfers

Your documents are not transferred anywhere by the app. Data handled by Google (ads, ML Kit diagnostics) may be processed in countries outside your own, including the United States, under Google's safeguards such as standard contractual clauses and, where available, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Encrypted files uploaded for “Anywhere” sharing are stored on Cloudflare's global network until they expire.

14. Coins, passes and purchases

  • Coins and passes are an in-app reward system. They are earned by watching optional rewarded videos or claimed as a small daily amount. They have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and are stored only on your phone.
  • Doculoom does not currently offer paid purchases or subscriptions. If paid features are added later, payment will be handled by Google Play Billing. We will not receive your card details, and we will update this policy before such a change.
